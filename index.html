FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about MEVO.

What is MEVO?

MEVO is a security-first crypto wallet architecture focused on proactive transaction and authorization protection.

What is MEVO building first?

Phase 1 focuses on the native MEVO Wallet for the EVM ecosystem.

What can MEVO inspect?

The security architecture is being designed around transaction payloads, smart-contract interactions, approvals, permits and signature requests.

Is MEVO available now?

The MEVO Wallet and browser extension are currently in development. Product availability will be announced as development progresses.

Which chains are part of Phase 1?

Phase 1 is EVM-first, including the planned Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon and Avalanche architecture. Non-EVM chains are not part of Phase 1.
