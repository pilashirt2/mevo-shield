Frequently Asked Questions
What is MEVO?
MEVO is a security-first crypto wallet project focused on proactive protection before dangerous blockchain actions are authorized.
What is MEVO building first?
Phase 1 is a native MEVO Wallet built for EVM networks. The architecture is being designed so additional chains can be explored in future phases.
What can MEVO inspect?
The security model is focused on transaction payloads, smart-contract interactions, token approvals, permit-style signatures and other authorization requests that may put wallet assets at risk.
Is MEVO available now?
MEVO Wallet is currently in development. Product, architecture and security research are being developed before public production deployment.
Which chains are supported?
Phase 1 is EVM-first. Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon and Avalanche represent the initial EVM ecosystem direction. Non-EVM chains are planned for future research and expansion, not claimed as live support today.
